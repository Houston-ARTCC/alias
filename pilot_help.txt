.BADREG .msg $aircraft Hello, the registration $aircraft is not a valid registration for an aircraft registered in the United States. Aircraft registrations in the U.S. start with an "N" and are comprised of 1-5 additional characters, with only the last two characters possibly being letters. The letters "I" and "O" are not valid as they can be easily confused with numbers. Here are a few valid registrations for example: N123AB, N1234A, N12345, N12AB, N123A, N1234, N1AB, N12A, N123, N1A, N12, N1. In the future, it is recommended that you login with a valid U.S. registration if you plan on flying with a N-registered aircraft. Thank you!

.BADRC .msg $aircraft Hello, please refrain from requesting a radio check unless absolutely necessary as it wastes valuable frequency time. For troubleshooting, visit https://vpilot.rosscarlson.dev/Documentation (vPilot) or https://docs.xpilot-project.org/docs (xPilot). Thank you!
